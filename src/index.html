How would you like to see IITG?

The same stops and the same stories, four ways. Buses stay on the through-roads and show some places from the road; walking and cycling tours go right in.

Version 4.0 · Final

What's new

  • A game guide and an Info keyPress F1 for the Game Guide: every place and thing on campus, what you can do there, and what works only in part or not at all. Press I anywhere and a panel tells you about the building or thing you are inside, near or looking at. (The OneStop phone is now on O.)
  • New menusA tabbed pause window (Game, Sound, Display & access, Controls, Progress & backup, About), tiles on the title screen, custom sliders and switches, and a clear backup/restore for your progress.
  • The hostel mess, properlyStudents queue, pay, take a steel plate, get served and eat; some go back for more. You show your mess card (your own hostel is free) or pay as a guest (₹50 breakfast, ₹75 lunch, ₹20 snacks, ₹75 dinner, ₹90 the Sunday special thali) in cash or by UPI, take a plate, hand in your coupon, and watch the plate fill with roti, sabji, dal, rice, curd, salad, papad and sweets.
  • OneStop growsCampus Live shows what is on right now at every place; buy a guest mess coupon in the app; the same menu and meal times everywhere.
  • Present on the big screenA presenter PC on the podium of the auditorium and of the Conference Centre: sign in (user iitg, password iitg) and show anything from the internet or YouTube. The auditorium screen now fills the whole stage opening.
  • IITG DUTY busesWhite, with a dark window band, a yellow-green stripe and the institute's name along the side; the front board reads IITG DUTY.
  • Campus map boardsThe “You are here” boards show the campus in the style of the printed map: grey roads, coloured buildings, green hills, blue lakes, red gates and an index, with a red marker where you stand.
  • Mown lawnsBright, short grass in front of the library, lecture halls, auditorium and other big buildings, with clipped round shrubs, rows of slim Ashoka trees, a big shade tree and a pine.
  • More to doBorrow and return library books (a fine if late); change into gym wear in the locker room and run on a treadmill; buy medicine at the hospital pharmacy; sit on every chair; knock over flower pots and boards and kick footballs.
  • Sound and musicNo more constant hiss: the campus is quiet until something happens. A room reverb, music that dips under the tour guide, and new tunes for the morning, the library, the mess and the rain.
  • Smoother pictureThe game draws about half as many triangles from the air and the start menu no longer flickers: trees change detail without flipping back and forth, leaves are anti-aliased, the camera's near plane follows its height, and the resolution drops a little on a slow graphics card instead of stuttering.
  • Hostel facilitiesEvery hostel has a library, a gym, a music room and a TV room on a floor of their own (the door on the east wall of the lobby), a wash area with mirrors and taps that run, a volleyball court, a basketball court and a cricket pitch. Boys' hostels let visitors in with the OneStop QR; girls' hostels ask everyone to sign the register.
  • Gates and entrancesThe campus gates keep only the OneStop QR desk, set beside the road; the Library and the Computer Centre keep theirs. The Main Gate has a small brick guard post, the café is just a café (Cafe Coffee Day), and Khokha Chai and Khokha Noodles stand outside Khokha Gate.
  • The Academic ComplexA podium with a broad flight of stone steps, a concrete portico, a glass entrance bay under the institute's name, stepped planters and a curved brick bed of red flowers, as in the photographs.
  • Present your own screenAt the presenter PC in the auditorium and the Conference Centre, press Present / share screen to put a window or browser tab of your own computer on the big screen (a YouTube tab that plays, slides, a document), as when a laptop is plugged into the projector. Press it again to stop.
  • Getting out of a vehicle, alwaysA Get off button now shows on the screen in every vehicle (E does the same). A second quick press of E no longer hops you straight back into the e-rickshaw, buggy, auto or taxi you have just left; a car or scooter brakes to a stop and lets you out even if you hold the throttle; and in the middle of the lake E swims you ashore. The KV Gate (Lothia Baghicha Gate on the campus map) now stands where the road from the circle meets the PWD Road, by the sub post office.
  • A proper pool, a school gate and a clearer wayThe swimming pool is a standard 25 m × 12.5 m pool with six lanes (it was as big as a hostel). The KV (Lothia Baghicha) Gate stands where the road from the circle by the school meets the PWD Road. Q now really hides the way to your objective, even in the first walkthrough. Bus shelters stand beside the road, never on it. Hostel graffiti carries only the hostel's own name or Assam: gamosa borders, a japi, Bihu, Kaziranga.
  • And moreDomino's Pizza and KFC side by side by the Food Court; a wall court by Dihing with washing hung out; a more detailed character (face, hands, shoes); a lake circle with a flower island, the air-quality board on two legs and the duck house behind it; the stage on the other end of the cricket ground; proper stands with steps; roads that meet cleanly.
  • FixesVehicles can drive in through the Main Gate again and never end up in a lake or on a ground; boards read from both sides and never cut off their text; nothing stands on a road.
