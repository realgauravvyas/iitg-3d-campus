<a id="gates"></a>
### Gates

_Main Gate · KV Gate · Khokha Gate_ · Main Gate 24 × 7 · others 6 AM – 10 PM

The Main Gate (Agiathuri) is open day and night, with its sandstone name stones in English, Assamese and Hindi, banded bollards and potted palms. The KV Gate (the Lothia Baghicha Gate on the campus map) stands where the road from the circle past the Kendriya Vidyalaya meets the PWD Road, by the sub post office; it has a brown IITG signboard, a steel gate on a tall pillar, the IIT sub post office built on to its wall and a cement guard post that was 3D printed. The Khokha Gate has two big steel gates between brick pillars, a boundary wall all round and a gravel path lined with parked cycles. KV and Khokha are open 6 AM to 10 PM. At each gate there is a desk with a OneStop QR machine, set on the verge beside the road (the road itself stays clear), where you scan in or out: it is the only way the gates keep a record, nobody writes you in or out. The Main Gate has a domed guard room and a small brick guard post with a guard on a chair.

## Everything that does not work

The same list the game shows under “What does not work”.

- **How to play**
  - ❌ Playing with other people online — Everyone else on campus is simulated.
  - ❌ Saving to the cloud — Use the menu’s backup if you want to carry your progress to another browser.
- **Info (I)**
  - 🟡 Small scenery such as single trees and lamp posts — They have no entry; you get the nearest place instead.
- **The campus**
  - 🟡 Real buildings and real heights — Footprints are from maps; heights are estimated; interiors are typical layouts, not the real floor plans.
  - 🟡 The real timetable, menu and people — They are made up to feel right.
  - ❌ The world beyond the gates as a playable area — The market, villages and river outside are there to look at and walk to.
- **On foot**
  - ❌ Walking through walls, fences or across lakes
  - ❌ Leaving the campus except through an open gate — Scan OneStop at the gate first.
- **Bicycle**
  - 🟡 Cycles on sports grounds, courts and the View Point — They are not allowed: the fences and gates stop them.
  - 🟡 Taking a cycle inside a building — Get off first; the door asks you to.
- **Campus bus**
  - 🟡 Boarding a shuttle — Only the tour bus can be ridden.
- **Rides & vehicles**
  - ❌ Driving on the sports grounds or into a lake — They are kept off.
  - ❌ Buying a car or a motorbike
- **Boats**
  - ❌ Fishing, or boats on the Brahmaputra
- **Drone**
  - 🟡 Flying inside buildings — The drone stays outdoors.
- **Campus tours**
  - 🟡 Every building on the route — Buses stay on the through-roads; walking and cycling tours go closer.
- **Gates**
  - 🟡 Khokha Gate — On foot or by cycle only.
  - ❌ The other two gates on the map (A.S.E.B and a south gate) — They are shown on the campus map and stay closed.
- **Bus stops**
  - 🟡 Riding the shuttles — Only the bus tour is rideable.
- **Cycle shop**
  - ❌ Repairs that cost money, or spare parts
- **Hostel**
  - 🟡 Visiting another hostel — A boys’ hostel: scan your OneStop QR at the door. A girls’ hostel: write your name in the register at the security desk (for the mess or the canteen); QR is only for its own residents. Common areas and the canteen only, never the rooms, at any time except 2 to 6 AM.
